Privacy Policy

PendoTrack — by Aceious  |  Effective Date: October 20, 2026

Summary: PendoTrack stores all your data locally on your device. Nothing is collected, tracked, or sent anywhere unless you explicitly choose to back up to Google Drive or export a note.

1. Introduction

PendoTrack ("the App") is a personal productivity tracker developed by Aceious ("we", "us", "our"). This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Android application.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. PendoTrack is designed with a privacy-first approach — your data belongs to you and stays on your device.

2. Information We Collect

2a. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

PendoTrack stores the following data locally on your device only, using on-device storage (Hive database and SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly initiate a backup or export:

  • Tasks (title, description, status, priority, due date, sub-tasks, notes)
  • Task categories, statuses, and priority levels you create
  • Personal notes and embedded drawings
  • Your display name (used for the dashboard greeting)
  • App preferences and settings

We do not have access to this data. It exists only on your device.

2b. Google Sign-In (Optional)

If you choose to use the Google Drive backup feature, you will be asked to sign in with your Google account. When you do, the App receives:

  • Your Google account display name
  • Your Google account email address
  • An authentication token to access your Google Drive

This information is used solely to authenticate the backup/restore process with Google Drive. We do not store your Google credentials, and we do not transmit this information to any server controlled by us.

2c. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage data
  • We do not collect device identifiers
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not use tracking cookies or pixels
  • We do not use any third-party analytics SDKs
  • We do not collect crash reports automatically

3. How Your Data Leaves the Device

Your data only leaves your device in two scenarios, both of which you initiate:

Scenario Where Data Goes What Data
Google Drive Backup Your personal Google Drive account (app-specific folder) All tasks, notes, categories, statuses, priorities, and settings
Note Export Android share sheet → destination of your choice A single note rendered as PNG or PDF

In both cases, you decide when and where data is sent. The App never transmits data in the background or without your explicit action.

4. Google Drive Integration

When you use the backup feature:

  • The App requests access to your Google Drive's app-specific data folder only
  • Backup files are stored in your own Google Drive, under a folder managed by PendoTrack
  • Only you have access to these files — we cannot see or access your Google Drive
  • You can delete the backup files at any time from your Google Drive
  • You can revoke the App's access to your Google Drive at any time via your Google Account permissions

5. Permissions

PendoTrack requests the following Android permissions:

Permission Purpose
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To show task reminders and scheduled notifications
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM To schedule task reminders at exact times
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED To restore scheduled reminders after device restart
INTERNET Required for Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup (optional features)

6. Third-Party Services

PendoTrack integrates with the following third-party services, only when you choose to use them:

  • Google Sign-In — for authentication when using Drive backup. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Drive API — for backup and restore. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not use any advertising SDKs, analytics platforms, or crash reporting tools.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data: Remains on your device until you delete it within the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.
  • Google Drive backups: Remain in your Google Drive until you delete them manually or revoke the App's access.
  • Exported notes: PNG/PDF files are placed in the App's cache directory and are cleared on the next App launch. Copies you share via the share sheet are under your control.

To delete all data:

  • Uninstall the App (removes all local data), or
  • Go to Android Settings → Apps → PendoTrack → Clear Data
  • Delete any Google Drive backups from your Drive

8. Children's Privacy

PendoTrack is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

9. Data Security

All data stored locally on your device is protected by your device's own security mechanisms (lock screen, encryption). All communication with Google services (Sign-In and Drive API) uses industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
